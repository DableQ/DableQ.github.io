FAQ

Common questions.

Does GhostGuard automatically decide that someone is cheating?

No. GhostGuard reports forensic findings, confidence and context. A staff member should review the evidence and apply the server's own rules.

Does GhostGuard delete or clean Windows artifacts?

No. The product is designed as a read-only forensic and integrity scanner. It does not exist to erase evidence.

How does licensing work?

A purchased license is activated through the GhostGuard launcher and can be restricted to a configured number of devices.

Do updates require downloading a new launcher every time?

No. The launcher can verify and obtain signed GhostGuard releases automatically.
